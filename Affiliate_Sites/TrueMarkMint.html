AUTHENTICITY VERIFICATION

Ten-layer certificate

Each NFT receives a comprehensive certificate comprising ten distinct verification layers. Redundant by design. Tamper-evident by mathematics.

01

Cryptographic hash (SHA-256)

Immutable fingerprint of the original asset. Any modification produces a completely different hash.

02

Creator digital signature

Elliptic curve signature proving creator authorization. Bound to wallet and timestamped.

03

Timestamp authority

Cryptographic proof of existence at a specific point in time, anchored to multiple timestamping services.

04

Storage location proof

Arweave transaction ID and IPFS content identifier permanently linking encrypted asset to location.

05

Encryption metadata

Complete record of ChaCha20-Poly1305 parameters: nonce, authentication tag, key derivation.

06

Visual fingerprint

Perceptual hash resistant to minor transformations while detecting meaningful alterations.

07

Provenance chain

Complete lineage from creation through all transfers, each transaction cryptographically linked.

08

Metadata integrity

Separate authentication of NFT metadata ensuring alignment with the asset.

09

Smart contract binding

Cryptographic commitment to the specific smart contract and token standard governing the NFT.

10

Certificate signature

Master signature binding all nine previous layers into a single, verifiable certificate issued by Spruked.
