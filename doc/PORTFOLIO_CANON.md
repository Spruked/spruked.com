# Spruked.com Portfolio Canon

Date: 2026-10-03
Status: Canonical product/content direction for the `portfolio-rebuild` branch.

## Purpose

Spruked.com is Bryan Spruk's master portfolio website.

Its job is to answer four questions clearly:

1. Who is Bryan Spruk?
2. What has he built?
3. What has he written or created?
4. Where can someone go to see each thing?

Spruked.com is not the product website for one individual system. It must not redefine Spruked around TrueMark, CertSig, knowledge objects, AI research, or any other single project.

Every meaningful project, system, book, business, design, research concept, or other body of work gets at least one dedicated portfolio page.

## Site-level rules

- Preserve the existing Spruked visual identity unless a deliberate design change is approved.
- Do not erase useful existing copy merely because it is on the wrong page. Reassign material to the appropriate portfolio entry where possible.
- Do not invent product status, launch status, customers, revenue, capabilities, certifications, legal claims, or technical validation.
- Every portfolio item must state its actual status.
- Distinguish active systems from prototypes, research, archived work, retired work, and superseded work.
- External projects with their own websites should receive a strong one-click external link from their Spruked portfolio page.
- Cart and checkout are not primary navigation. They may remain in the codebase for future use but should not define the portfolio experience.
- Do not collapse separate projects into a synthetic business story merely because they share technology or concepts.
- Do not treat repository contents as permission to redefine company hierarchy or brand meaning.

## Portfolio item contract

Every dedicated portfolio page should provide, when information exists:

1. Project / work name
2. Category
3. Status
4. What it is
5. Why Bryan built or wrote it
6. The problem it addresses or purpose it serves
7. Bryan's contribution / authorship
8. How it works at a high level
9. Key architecture, ideas, or innovations
10. Creation date / major milestones
11. Screenshots, demonstrations, documents, excerpts, or other evidence
12. Related projects / works
13. External website, GitHub repository, demo, publication, or source link if applicable

## Brand: SPRUKED U

The SPRUKED U / falling diacritic is a meaningful work in its own right and should be documented in the portfolio.

Its portfolio treatment should explain:

- the long-U pronunciation of Spruk
- the repeated collapse of that vowel in ordinary pronunciation
- the upright U as the intended/correct form
- the falling diacritic as the distortion entering the system
- the mark as a phonetic event represented geometrically
- the relationship between distortion, correction, signal, noise, identity, and truth
- the relationship of the glyph to the broader Spruked / Pro Prime / ORB identity system

Do not reduce this to a generic logo-gallery entry.

## Migration principle

The current Spruked.com repository contains useful material, but much of it is arranged under the wrong parent story.

Migration should therefore favor:

- preserve
- reclassify
- relocate
- rewrite only where necessary

rather than indiscriminate deletion.

Examples:

- TrueMark content becomes TrueMark portfolio content.
- CertSig content becomes CertSig / Alpha CertSig portfolio content.
- ORB, A.I.M.S., cognition, memory, and governance material can support their respective portfolio entries.
- Orb Weaver, Web Weaver, Code Weaver, POPS, and other external projects should link prominently to their own properties when available.
- Books become first-class portfolio entries rather than secondary mentions.
